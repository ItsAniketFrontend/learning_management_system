How a tuition request becomes a paid class

A parent posts what their child needs. Our admin team checks the request and picks the right tutor. The tutor takes it through a demo class to regular tuition. This page shows each step and who does it.

Who is involved

Parent / student

Posts a requirement in about a minute. No account needed; the phone number is verified by OTP.

Admin team

Calls every new lead, chooses the tutor, and follows up until the tuition starts.

Tutor

Keeps a verified profile, receives matched leads, and accepts or declines within 24 hours.

Automatic alerts

WhatsApp and SMS messages the system sends at each hand-off, so nobody has to check the site.

      To decide before we build

      These choices change parts of the flow above.

      • Revenue modelTutor subscription or credits, a commission on the first month's fee, or a parent registration fee. This decides when the parent's number unlocks (step 10).
      • Launch scopeHome tuition only, or online classes too. One city at launch, or several.
      • Assignment styleOne tutor at a time, or a shortlist of 3 that the parent chooses from (step 7).
      • Reply window24 hours is suggested for tutors to accept. A shorter window gets parents matched faster.
