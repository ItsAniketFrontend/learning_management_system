Tuitionpedia · product workflow

How Tuitionpedia turns a parent's request into a tuition

A parent posts a requirement. The system checks it and finds matching tutors. The admin team reviews the match, interested tutors unlock the parent's contact with their plan credits, and the platform tracks the demo through to a placed tuition and ongoing support.

Who is involved

Parent / student

Searches tutors or posts a requirement, verifies their mobile by OTP, shortlists tutors and books a demo.

Tuitionpedia system

Validates requests, matches tutors, sends WhatsApp/SMS/email alerts, runs deadlines and reminders, and handles credits.

Admin & support

Reviews matches, verifies tutors, follows up with parents, handles payments, tickets and complaints from one dashboard.

Tutor

Builds a verified profile, responds to lead alerts, unlocks contacts with plan credits and takes demo classes.

        Support, complaints and safety

        Support stays available after placement. Parents and tutors can report a problem or block the other side at any time.

        1. Ticket or complaint raisedBy parent or tutor, with a category, details and optional evidence.
        2. Owner assignedSupport picks it up and a response time (SLA) starts.
        3. InvestigationSupport talks to both sides and keeps notes. Overdue tickets escalate to admin.
        4. ResolutionClosed with a satisfaction rating, or:Replacement tutorAccount suspended for review

        Published safety guidance asks for a parent or guardian at first meetings, checking the tutor's identity, and agreeing fees and timings in writing.

        What the system does automatically

        Messages respect each person's consent and opt-out settings, and are never sent twice for the same event.
